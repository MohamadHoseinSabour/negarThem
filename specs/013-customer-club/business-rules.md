## 3. Worked Financial Examples (Provisional No-Fee Model)

> All figures below are in integer Iranian Toman (`IRT`) strictly for verifying formula invariants under the provisional no-fee model.

### Example 1 — Exact Division (`negar_aghaz`, Purchase at Ceiling)
- **Plan:** `negar_aghaz` (Initial: `3,000,000` IRT, Ceiling: `6,000,000` IRT, Term: `3` months)
- **Actual Purchase Amount:** `6,000,000` IRT
- **Applied Initial Payment:** `3,000,000` IRT
- **Remaining Principal:** `6,000,000 - 3,000,000 = 3,000,000` IRT
- **Installments (3 months):** `[1,000,000, 1,000,000, 1,000,000]` IRT
- **Reconciliation Check:** `sum(installments) = 3,000,000` IRT; `total_payable = 3,000,000 + 3,000,000 = 6,000,000` IRT (exact match).

### Example 2 — Non-Divisible Remainder (`negar_plus`, Purchase at Ceiling)
- **Plan:** `negar_plus` (Initial: `5,000,000` IRT, Ceiling: `10,000,000` IRT, Term: `6` months)
- **Actual Purchase Amount:** `10,000,000` IRT
- **Applied Initial Payment:** `5,000,000` IRT
- **Remaining Principal:** `10,000,000 - 5,000,000 = 5,000,000` IRT
- **Exact Quotient:** `5,000,000 ÷ 6 = 833,333.333...` IRT (not an integer)
- **Under Proposed Integer Rounding (1-IRT precision, final installment absorbs remainder):**
  - Installments 1–5: `833,333` IRT each (`5 × 833,333 = 4,166,665` IRT)
  - Installment 6: `5,000,000 - 4,166,665 = 833,335` IRT
  - Sum: `4,166,665 + 833,335 = 5,000,000` IRT (zero drift).
- **Under Alternative 1,000-IRT Step Rounding (final installment absorbs remainder):**
  - Installments 1–5: `833,000` IRT each (`5 × 833,000 = 4,165,000` IRT)
  - Installment 6: `5,000,000 - 4,165,000 = 835,000` IRT
  - Sum: `4,165,000 + 835,000 = 5,000,000` IRT (zero drift, cleaner Persian payment amounts).

### Example 3 — Purchase Below Plan Ceiling (`negar_vizhe`, Purchase `14,000,000` IRT)
- **Plan:** `negar_vizhe` (Initial: `10,000,000` IRT, Ceiling: `20,000,000` IRT, Term: `6` months)
- **Actual Purchase Amount:** `14,000,000` IRT *(assuming `CLUB-BD-005` allows purchases below ceiling with fixed initial payment)*
- **Applied Initial Payment:** `10,000,000` IRT *(note: if proportional 50% down payment were chosen instead in `CLUB-BD-005`, initial payment would be `7,000,000` IRT — hence why `CLUB-BD-005` must be decided explicitly)*
- **Remaining Principal (Fixed-Initial Model):** `14,000,000 - 10,000,000 = 4,000,000` IRT
- **Installments (1-IRT precision):** 5 × `666,666` IRT + final `666,670` IRT = `4,000,000` IRT.

### Example 4 — Boundary: Purchase Below Initial Payment (`negar_aghaz`, Purchase `2,500,000` IRT)
- **Plan Initial Payment:** `3,000,000` IRT > **Actual Purchase Amount:** `2,500,000` IRT.
- **Invariant:** System MUST NOT calculate `2,500,000 - 3,000,000 = -500,000` IRT.
- **Result:** Either rejected as below minimum installment threshold (recommended) or treated as `0` installment financing (full cash purchase `2,500,000` IRT).

### Example 5 — Boundary: Purchase Above Plan Ceiling (`negar_plus`, Purchase `12,500,000` IRT)
- **Plan Ceiling:** `10,000,000` IRT, **Plan Credit Portion:** `5,000,000` IRT.
- **Option A (Disallow excess — `CLUB-BD-006`):** Checkout rejects `negar_plus` and suggests `negar_vizhe` (`20,000,000` IRT ceiling).
- **Option B (Allow excess paid upfront — `CLUB-BD-006`):** Required upfront payment = `5,000,000` (plan initial) + `2,500,000` (excess over ceiling) = `7,500,000` IRT; `remaining_principal` remains strictly capped at `5,000,000` IRT.

### Example 6 — Plan Price Modified After Agreement Creation
- Customer signs `CreditAgreement #104` under `negar_plus` (`5,000,000` initial, `10,000,000` ceiling, `6` months).
- Two weeks later, administrator changes `negar_plus` to `6,000,000` initial, `12,000,000` ceiling, `4` months.
- **Result:** `CreditAgreement #104` continues operating strictly on its frozen snapshot (`6` installments summing to `5,000,000` IRT). Only new applications/agreements after the edit use the updated settings.
